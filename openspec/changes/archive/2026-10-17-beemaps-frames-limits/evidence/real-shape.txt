A check of the route and the layer with fake answers of the shape of the one real call of 2026-10-16. No server was called.
The script real-shape.mjs lives outside the repository, because a script in a change folder is a coverage gap.
It gives the route a fake fetch function that answers a document with the top-level keys frames, cost, creditsUsed, creditsRemaining and notEnoughCredits.
The values are creditsUsed 99, creditsRemaining -0.25, cost 0 and notEnoughCredits true. The frames have the fields of the real frames.
The route writes its log lines through the option log, and the script prints them. Then the script gives the answer of the route to the function normalizeAnswer of the layer.
The fake frames have about 263 bytes, and the real frames have about 486 bytes. So the largest fake document is smaller than a real one of the same count.
