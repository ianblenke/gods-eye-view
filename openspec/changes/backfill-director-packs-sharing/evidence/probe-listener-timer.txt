Base commit: 290b5d2.
Known limit: session-listener-timer.

The caller signal cancels the load call during listener registration.
The session clears that load call before it creates its timer.
The timer stays after the caller destroys the session.
The test uses fixed timer tokens to avoid a live timer.
This probe does not support an equivalent claim.
